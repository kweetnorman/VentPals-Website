Why we are here

A recognized 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit

Children deserve to understand themselves before the world tells them who to be.

VentPals is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit focused on emotional literacy, self-expression, and trusted connection. We believe children deserve room to name what they feel, explore what those feelings mean, and practice using their voice.

Our approach is preventative and educational—not therapy, diagnosis, or a replacement for professional care. We create supportive tools that can complement the caring people and resources already in a child's life.
